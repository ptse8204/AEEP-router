  exposure, differential inventories, four arms and reviewed reusable artifacts.

  schedules, and previews route-specific trial allowances and time ceilings.

  and can commit with scope/review changes in one transaction.
- Reports enforce predeclared benefit dimensions, 10% resource/5pp success
  thresholds, 10% regression limits, adjusted grouped confidence intervals and
  feasible challenger dominance. Unknown discovery prevents catalog admission.
- Differential conformance supplements each worker's enforcement records and is
  rechecked for scoped production use. Configured and effective policy differ.
- Optional skill treatment omits forced invocation. Unknown selection/schema
  measurements remain unknown. Reviewed worker files are checked inside images.
- Capable shared and treatment Linux images passed actual unauthenticated sandbox
  dependency checks. Their source observations are in incremental-worker-review.json.
- The workbook executable recipe has 8/28/105 cases, seven variation groups,
  separate OOXML grading, literal fixtures, six plausible faults, deterministic
  rendering, supported-formula cache checks and bounded structural extraction.
- The complete contained workbook path materializes cases, validates its grader,
  retains operation accounting and reports a deliberately wrong candidate as
  unsuitable. Reviewed recipe programs use literal argv; artifact validation is
  batched within the existing input limits. This is a native controlled fixture,
  not model-driven Spreadsheets evidence.
- Managed App Server workers are closed and their containers removed after every
  invocation, including failures. Unconfirmed cleanup cannot report success.
- New schemas, packaged build/recipe assets, repository instructions and separate
  product/upstream-support gates are included.
