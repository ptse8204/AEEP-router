in every treatment turn. The experiment measures access to the skill; it does
not establish how its text influenced an individual repair.

Plain and gzip output conditions remain separate. Reported input-plus-output
tokens totaled 1334530 versus 1691894 in plain, and 683419 versus 753147 in gzip.
The small observed wall-time differences do not establish supported savings.
Cached tokens and reasoning tokens are retained separately, without adding them
again to those totals. Cash savings are unknown.

The [failure diagnosis](FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md) shows remaining bug tests in four
failed repair cases and preservation failures in the fifth. Partial progress did
not meet the frozen success endpoint. The two earlier controller-censored cases
remain excluded, with all three model turns charged.

The actual admission API refused both development reports. Qualification was
absent and measured benefit was not established. Those refusals demonstrate this
guard for these reports; they do not validate every ranking or abstention decision.
No production capability was admitted or activated.

This is a completed comparative development result with an inconclusive general
usefulness verdict. It supplies a reason to retain the ordinary baseline here.
A positive live selection benefit remains unproven. More infrastructure tests
cannot supply that missing evidence, and repeated trials on these exposed tasks
would not create independent holdouts. A further efficacy study needs a justified
capability/workload hypothesis, an untouched cohort, and applicable execution
authority before dispatch. No thresholds or historical outcomes were changed.
