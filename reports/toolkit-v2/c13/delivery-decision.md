# Local delivery decision

The local evaluation stack is implemented and its first completed four-arm model
comparison is available. The evidence supports a bounded local experimental
handoff. Public release and production benefit claims are NO_GO while the human
check and broader resource/applicability requirements remain unmet. This is an
engineering recommendation; no operator release decision or publication is
implied.

## Completed comparison and resource evidence

All four arms passed 105/105 held-out cases. Optional Ponytail versus the ordinary
agent has zero task-success gain and a paired wall estimate of 0.79%, with
corrected bounds -4.61% to +6.52%. This does not meet the frozen 10% benefit
threshold. The strict canonical verdict is INCONCLUSIVE because trusted token
pairs are absent. Actual candidate exposure/use and generated-tool invocation
are unknown. No automatic admission or verified savings follows.

[The adjudication](../c07/edit-value-native-20261019/adjudication.md) explains the
scope and failed preparation. The linked study ledger retains 2,836 unique
operations and all known durations, including qualification, builder, training,
images, readiness failures, the cancelled 46-call attempt and cleanup. Charged
operation intervals overlap; their sum is not study elapsed time. One initial
metadata observation lacked advance reservation and remains separately recorded.

The [footprint inventory](../integration-completion/delivery-footprint-20261019.json)
records the shipped toolkit package/integration files, historical evidence-driver
archive, the 33-wheel package closure and 76,125,317 preview disk bytes. Those
artifact-derived preview bounds do not measure whole-host memory, energy or all
maintenance. The reused legacy repository and external software have additional
costs. The [image inventory](../integration-completion/delivery-images-20261019.jsonl)
records exact tags/IDs and logical sizes; shared layers make a sum misleading.
No image, volume or backup was removed here.
