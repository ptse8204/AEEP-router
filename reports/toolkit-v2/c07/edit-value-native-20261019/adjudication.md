# Editing comparison adjudication

The completed comparison does not demonstrate that optional Ponytail improves
these tasks. Every arm passed all 105 held-out cases. Candidate versus ordinary
agent success gain is zero; its paired wall-time estimate is 0.79%, with a
multiplicity-corrected interval of -4.61% to +6.52%. This does not meet the frozen
10% resource-benefit threshold. Higher effort and the generated tool also lack a
confidence-supported wall-time benefit over the candidate.

The canonical verdict remains `insufficient_evidence` (INCONCLUSIVE), rather
than a complete negative utility verdict, because the declared token guardrails
have no observed/verified pairs. All 564 protocol input/output reports are present
with `provider_report/self_asserted` provenance. Their raw counts remain useful
descriptions, without a verified token-savings or cash-savings claim. Candidate
exposure, retrieval and invocation fields are unknown in all 105 held-out
candidate receipts. Image/inventory conformance verifies the intended difference;
it cannot establish actual candidate use. Generated-tool invocation is also
unmeasured. Analysis includes all assigned cases and never selects on invocation.

This result applies to seven bounded templates in each of three families:
one-file literal editing, mechanical Python token renames across three files,
and adjacent log-prefix counting. It does not establish arbitrary debugging,
programming competence, or the skill's value on other tasks or hosts. No source,
task, grader, threshold, effort or exposure was tuned after holdout. No automatic
admission or candidate activation occurred.

Qualification separately passed 141 tasks including 105 disjoint holdouts.
Generated-tool validation reused only its 28 construction training inputs and is
not qualification. The pre-holdout partial value attempt retains its 46 calls;
failed readiness/profile plans and image packaging attempts remain recorded.

and 9,924.50 known charged operation seconds. Those intervals overlap and are
not elapsed study wall time. One initial unreserved metadata observation remains
separate at 0.112110291 tool wall seconds. Engineering and whole-host resource
costs are incomplete; no verified break-even follows.
